# The Keystone HPC Platform

High-performance computing centers all face a common challenge: how to
allocate a limited pool of IT resources across large user groups with rapidly
evolving demands. This makes a few jobs unavoidable. Someone has to decide how
the capacity is shared, confirm it is being used the way it was intended, and
account for what it ultimately produced. As a center grows and takes on more
users, these stop being occasional tasks and become the everyday work of
keeping a center running.

Traditionally, each of these jobs is handled independently. Resource
allocation is managed in one place, monitoring in another, and reporting is
stitched together afterward from whatever records happen to exist. Each tool
performs well individually, but bridging the gaps between them creates 
friction and overhead. This disjointed workflow puts unneccessary strain on
administrators and translates into a worse customer experience for HPC users.

Keystone solves this problem by providing a unified management platform for
HPC resources. Users get self-service access to request resources and monitor
team consumption. Administrators get a central control plane for setting 
allocation policy, monitoring usage in real time, and tracing that usage 
back to individual teams and projects. Because allocation and access are 
governed together, the two never drift apart. Keystone's built-in automation 
keeps every change in sync, so what Keystone records and what the 
machines permit are always the same thing.
